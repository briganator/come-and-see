Come and See

The story of Joseph Smith and the Restoration

“Nathanael said unto him, Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth? Philip saith unto him, Come and see.”John 1:46

This is the story of a farm family in New England and New York, of a boy who read a verse in James and went into the woods to pray, and of what he said happened there and afterward. It is told as a narrative, from the records the people themselves left: Lucy Mack Smith's history, Joseph Smith's own accounts, letters, journals, and the Church's published histories. Where a scene detail is imagined, the book says so with a small mark (†) and a note.

It is written for Latter-day Saints, for friends of other faiths, and with special tenderness for Jewish readers, whose prophets Elijah and Malachi run through these pages. No one is asked to agree. The invitation is Philip's: come and see.
