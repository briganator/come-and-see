Visit the Sites

These historic sites are open to visitors and maintained by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Addresses are taken from each site’s official page (checked October 2026). Hours change by season, so check the official page before you go.

Joseph Smith Birthplace Memorial

357 LDS Lane, South Royalton, Vermont 05068

Where Joseph Smith was born on December 23, 1805, in a home on Solomon Mack's farm. A 50-foot granite monument raised in 1905 marks the site.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 3

Smith Family Farm and the Sacred Grove

843 Stafford Rd, Palmyra, New York 14522

The Smith log home, the frame home Alvin began, and the grove where Joseph prayed in the spring of 1820. Missionaries guide the homes; the grove is self-guided.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 5

Hill Cumorah

603 State Route 21, Palmyra, New York 14522

The hill where Joseph met the angel Moroni each year from 1823 and received the plates on September 22, 1827. A visitors’ center stands at its base.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 6

Priesthood Restoration Site

27832 State Route 171, Oakland Township (Susquehanna), Pennsylvania 18847

Harmony, where most of the Book of Mormon was translated and where the Aaronic Priesthood was restored on May 15, 1829. Reconstructed homes of Joseph and Emma and of Isaac and Elizabeth Hale.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 7

Peter Whitmer Farm

1451 Aunkst Rd, Waterloo, New York 13165 (visitors’ center at 1455 Aunkst Rd)

Where the translation was finished in June 1829 and the Church was organized on April 6, 1830.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 8

Historic Kirtland

Visitors’ center: 7800 Kirtland-Chardon Rd, Kirtland, Ohio 44094

The Newel K. Whitney store, where Joseph arrived on February 4, 1831, with restored homes and mills nearby.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 8

Kirtland Temple

Visitors’ center: 7809 Joseph St, Kirtland, Ohio 44094

Dedicated March 27, 1836. Guided tours begin at the visitors’ center every half hour, limited to 25 people.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 9

Liberty Jail

216 North Main St, Liberty, Missouri 64068

The original jail no longer stands. The visitors’ center holds a full-scale model of it, built to show its size and construction.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 10

Historic Nauvoo

North Visitors’ Center: 290 Main St, Nauvoo, Illinois 62354 · South Visitors’ Center: 865 Water St

The city the Saints built from 1839 to 1846, with restored homes and shops, the Smith family homestead and Joseph’s Red Brick Store.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 11

Carthage Jail

310 Buchanan St, Carthage, Illinois 62321

Where Joseph and Hyrum Smith were killed on June 27, 1844. Tours begin with a short orientation and video in the visitors’ center.

Official site, hours and tours · Directions · On our map · Chapter 12
